## ✨ What it does

Pocket Browser is a small, calm home for the links you want to keep. There are no accounts, no clutter, and nothing to configure. You paste a link, it lands on your shelf, and it's there the next time you open the app.

## 📌 Features

| | |
|---|---|
| 🔗 **One-step saving** | Paste any web address and hit **File it**. No need to type `https://`. |
| 📁 **Folders** | Sort links into your own folders, like *Thesis sources*, *Recipes* or *Read later*. Anything you haven't sorted waits in **Unsorted**. |
| 🔍 **Instant search** | Type a few letters to filter your whole collection by title or site name. |
| ✏️ **Rename and organize** | Give any entry a better title, move it to another folder, or remove it when you're done. |
| 🎨 **Easy to scan** | Each entry gets a color-coded tag with the site's initial, its domain, the date you saved it, and its folder. |
| 🌗 **Light and dark** | Follows your system theme automatically. |
| 🔒 **Private by design** | Your collection stays on your own device. No sign-up, no tracking, no server. |
| 🌐 **Opens in your browser** | Click a saved link and it opens in your usual browser. |

## 🗺️ How it works

1. **Paste** a link into the bar at the top.
2. **File it** into the folder you're viewing, or leave it in Unsorted.
3. **Come back later.** Browse by folder or search, then click to open.

## 🧭 A tidy shelf

- **All sites** shows everything you've saved, newest first.
- **Unsorted** shows links that haven't been given a folder yet.
- **Your folders** each show a count, so you can see at a glance what's in them.
- Deleting a folder never deletes your links. They move safely to **Unsorted**.
